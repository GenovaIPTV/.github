> 🎉 **Now on Google Play** — install the stable release, or opt into the public beta to get new features first.
> Genova is free to use.

Watch live TV, movies, and series from **your own** IPTV provider. Genova is a player only — bring your
Xtream Codes credentials or an M3U / M3U8 playlist and Genova organizes the rest. Your playlists and
settings stay on your device; no account, no tracking.

## What it does

- **Live TV & EPG** — browse channels with an integrated Electronic Program Guide
- **VOD & Series** — movies and TV series, organized by category
- **Smart search** — find any channel, movie, or show in real time
- **Multiple providers** — Xtream Codes, M3U, and M3U8, switchable instantly
- **Built for every screen** — Material Design 3 on Android, with a native Apple TV experience (10-foot TV UI) in
  development
